Section · 01 / Core Principles

Seven principles for responsible technology.

Guiding principles to support ethical and responsible technology development and deployment across the ecosystem.

Showing 7 of 7
Last reviewed · Apr 2026
PRINCIPLE / 01

Human-Centricity, Reliability & Safety

Prioritising human well-being while ensuring systems are robust, secure, and safe throughout their lifecycle.

P-01 / SAFETY
Lifecycle Robustness Well-being
Read principle
Define safe operating envelopes, monitor performance, and ensure human oversight at decision points where consequences are material.
PRINCIPLE / 02

Data Privacy, Security & Governance

Ensuring responsible data management, protection, and compliance with applicable laws and standards.

P-02 / PDPA
PDPAData Stewardship
Read principle
Apply privacy-by-design, minimise data collection, and align controls to recognised security frameworks and PDPA obligations.
PRINCIPLE / 03

Societal Benefit & Sustainability

Promoting technologies that deliver positive societal impact and support long-term sustainability.

P-03 / IMPACT
Public InterestESG
Read principle
Evaluate downstream effects on communities and the environment; prefer solutions whose value compounds responsibly over time.
PRINCIPLE / 04

Fairness & Bias Mitigation

Actively identifying and addressing bias to ensure equitable and non-discriminatory outcomes.

P-04 / EQUITY
Bias AuditEquity
Read principle
Audit training data and outputs for disparate impact; surface and remediate biases discovered in development and production.
PRINCIPLE / 05

Inclusive Design & Risk Awareness

Designing systems that are inclusive and accessible, while proactively identifying and managing potential risks.

P-05 / A11Y
AccessibilityRisk Register
Read principle
Embed accessibility from the start; maintain a living risk register that names owners and mitigation paths.
PRINCIPLE / 06

Explainability & Transparency

Enabling clear understanding of system processes and decision-making to support trust and accountability.

P-06 / XAI
Model CardsDisclosure
Read principle
Publish model cards, decision rationales and known limitations so stakeholders can interrogate behaviour with confidence.
PRINCIPLE / 07

Accountability & Auditability

Establishing clear responsibility and enabling traceability through effective governance and audit mechanisms — so every consequential decision can be traced to a person, a process, and a record.
